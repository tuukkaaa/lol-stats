 Welcome to the official patch of the 2025 League of Legends World Championship! As a result, we're delivering some buffs for champions who underperform when comparing experienced players to experienced players of other champions, some nerfs to long-term overperformers in lower skill brackets, and a set of adjustments aimed at buffing or nerfing pro power without affecting overall win rates for Normal and Ranked play.

Streamer Mode Improvements
 Griefing in high MMR games is a problem we see pretty consistently that ruins games for players, specifically for pros, streamers, or content creators.

Sure it's cool to get into a game with your favorite streamer or pro, but what isn't cool are the players that run it down in these players' games to get a cheap laugh, camping these player's lanes, or just generally altering how you play because of a high profile player in your game. This absolutely isn't something we're okay with, so we've built an upgraded streamer mode in partnership with our creators and pros to protect against negative actions like these.

In practice, our new and improved streamer mode will have three modes of anonymity that you can opt into. They are as follows: 
Hide Other Names: This will hide players' game names on your screen only. Your identity will still be visible to other players in game.
Hide My Name: This will hide your RiotID and Game Name to all other players in champ select, in game, and in third party apps, but your account progression (Mastery Score, Challenges, Title, Profile Pic, Ranked Border, etc.) will still be visible.
Hide My Everything: This will hide your RiotID, Game Name, and all of your account progression to all other players in champ select, in game, and in third party apps. If you're looking to be anonymous then this is the mode you'll want to select.
Banning Allied Hovers [NA & OCE ONLY]
 In situations where your "teammate" bans your champion we see higher report rates, lower win rates, and a reported overall worse experience. In this patch we're experimenting with removing the ability to ban your ally's hovered champion to help alleviate these negative interactions. We'll be watching how this change goes and listening to your feedback before making a call on whether we revert this change or roll it out worldwide, so please let us know what you think! 
In Normal Draft, Ranked, and Arena (or any other queue with a ban phase), you can no longer ban a champion an ally is hovering.

 Anivia's been struggling to find success in the mid lane as of late. Her low mobility is one of her weaknesses, so we're lowering her Q's cooldown slightly as a way for her to keep her enemies at bay while also giving her a bit more damage on her E to help her out.

Azir
 Azir's a bit too powerful in higher levels of play at the moment, but he's in pretty good shape for regular play. So we're looking to shift his power around and reduce those pro-specific strengths. To do this, we're digging into his early laning phase and giving him less passive guaranteed wave control and compensating him with better late-game scaling and more rewards for taking big risks.

While she appears powerful in the hands of most players we don't think this shape is healthy for her as a whole, so we're giving her W some additional power that will give her some more strength to play around early while balancing that with reduced base stats, which shouldn't feel particularly noticeable.

Fizz
 Fizz is a little weak right now (some might say he's floundering) and is incredibly reliant on snowballing, as his base damages are appropriate in the early game but generally don't scale well without gold. We're looking to make some power-positive adjustments to make him less reliant on snowballing while still asking him to build primarily AP assassin items. As a whole, these changes should be positive for his up-front W burst damage, make his ultimate a bit weaker, and open up a few new item choices.

Graves
 Graves is a fairly popular jungler that's performing decently right now, but his builds have devolved into one-shot lethality. We don't believe this to be a good state for him because his playstyle becomes one-shot or be one-shot which isn't fun for anyone. We think it'd be better for him to have a sustained combat pattern where skillful Graves players can utilize their Grit, so we're nudging his builds and playstyle around to make him less reliant on bonus AD. Overall this should net out in a small buff once this change has had some time to settle and players adapt.

We're okay with his sustain, but to bring his DPS down just a tad we're hitting his AP ratios which should weaken some of his most popular builds while leaving his AD builds untouched.

Lane Swap Detection
 It's important that players have a fair fight in their early laning phases, both in pro play and everywhere else. Thankfully, our more recent lane swap mitigation tactics have removed 2v1 lanes at the start of game from pro play while generally not intruding on everyone else. In the interest of cultivating exciting and non-deterministic laning phases this Worlds, we're extending lane swap detection for another minute in top lane.

As this mechanic only affects support champions venturing to the far side of the map, this won't affect your level 1 Aurelion Sol roams… unless you're playing ASol support. 
Top Lane Detection: Until 3:00 ⇒ Until 4:00
